# Purpose
The provided content outlines a structured approach for documenting a large software codebase by breaking it down into manageable subsets. The purpose of this configuration is to guide a software engineering expert in synthesizing detailed descriptions of these subsets into a comprehensive overview of the entire codebase. This involves writing a cohesive narrative that explains the codebase's purpose, functionality, and usage, while also identifying the main business logic and critical APIs. The document emphasizes the importance of accuracy and relevance, instructing the expert to include only the most pertinent information derived from the given descriptions, without introducing any speculative content. This ensures that the final documentation is both informative and reliable, serving as a valuable resource for understanding the codebase's architecture and functionality.
