# Purpose
The provided content outlines a template for generating a concise summary of a software codebase based on detailed descriptions of its components. The template is designed to guide a software engineering expert in crafting a single-sentence description that encapsulates the entire codebase. It specifies that the expert will receive detailed descriptions of various files and folders within the codebase, including their names, paths, and specific descriptions. The expert's task is to synthesize this information into a coherent summary sentence that includes the name of the codebase and references to the key files or folders described. This structured approach ensures that the summary is accurate, concise, and directly derived from the provided information, without any speculative or extraneous content.
