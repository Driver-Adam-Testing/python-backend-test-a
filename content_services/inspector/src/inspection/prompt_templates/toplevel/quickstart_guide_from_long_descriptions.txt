You will be given detailed descriptions of the modules (files and folders) that make up the codebase.

5) Be terse and direct.
6) Use neutral language and do not comment on the quality of the codebase (good or bad).
7) Provide your response in only 4 paragraphs.

Do not make up information. Only write content derived from the information given to you.
